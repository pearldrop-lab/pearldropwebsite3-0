Aerial & drone

A view of your place nobody has had.

Some things simply cannot be photographed from the ground. The whole site in one frame. A building sitting in its landscape. A showground at the moment it fills up. That is what the camera goes up for.

We have been flying for clients since 2014, and the work is nearly always the same handful of shots done properly: the slow reveal off the front of a building, the tracking move down a drive, the pull-back that shows how much land there actually is. Shot in 4K, with stills from the same flight, and graded to match the rest of your film rather than looking like something bolted on afterwards.

Most of our aerials are added to a shoot we are already booked for. We are on site with the cameras anyway, so the air becomes half a day on top rather than a second company, a second call-out and a second invoice. It is a fraction of what a helicopter costs and, for the shots most clients want, it looks better.

Before we quote we check the airspace and whatever permissions the location needs, and come back with a straight answer. We are JOSCAR accredited — the supply-chain standard for aerospace, defence and security — so if your site runs to a gate, a badge and a briefing, we have been through that before.
