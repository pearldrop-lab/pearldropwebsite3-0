### 2 — Body

**H2: Two minutes to explain what you actually do**

The old company profile was eleven minutes long, opened on a slow push into
reception and featured the MD reading from a lectern. It exists somewhere on
your server and nobody has watched it since 2014. Fair enough.

The version that works now is two minutes, front-loaded, and built out of your
actual premises: the machine that only three people in the country can operate,
the whiteboard nobody has cleaned since March, the team meeting at 8:15 that
tells a visitor more about your culture than any values statement. We shoot it
over one or two days, mostly handheld and mostly on the move, with interviews
short enough that people forget they're being interviewed.

There's a practical payoff too. A film on your homepage keeps visitors on the
page longer, which search engines notice, and it gives the sales team something
to send that isn't another PDF. The same shoot feeds the recruitment page, the
tender submission and the reception screen.

We'll happily film in our Stevenage studio against the greenscreen or the white
cove — but for a company profile, your own building almost always wins.
