### 2 — Body

**H2: A podcast people watch, not one they leave on in the background**

The default video podcast is a meeting room, a pot plant, and two people leaning
into microphones under a strip light. It sounds fine. It looks like a video call
someone forgot to end.

We'd rather shoot yours somewhere worth looking at. That might be a studio with
a set built for the series, our greenscreen and infinity cove in Stevenage, or a
real place with some character to it — a distillery, a workshop, a members' club.
The room is doing half the work before anyone says a word, and it is the single
biggest difference between a vodcast that looks like a brand and one that looks
like a hobby.

Then the boring craft that makes it watchable. Three or four cameras running
continuously, so the edit always has somewhere to cut. A separate mic and a
separate audio track per person, so one cough doesn't cost you the best answer
in the episode. Lighting that flatters guests who are not actors and are quietly
terrified.

Most clients bank a run of episodes in a day — same set, same lighting, one
crew call — which is what makes a series affordable rather than a nice idea that
stops after episode two.

### 3 — Great for
