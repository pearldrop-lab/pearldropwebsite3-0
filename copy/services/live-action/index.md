### 2 — Body

**H2: Everything that involves a camera and a Tuesday**

Most people arrive here knowing they need a film and not knowing what it's
called. An awards ceremony and a product close-up are both live-action, and
they need almost nothing in common — one is six cameras and a vision mixer,
the other is a macro lens and four hours of nobody breathing on the glass.

So this page is a map, not a pitch. Underneath it sit fourteen services, each
with its own page, its own examples and its own price bracket. Pick the one
that sounds like your problem and go straight there.

What they share is us: a Hertfordshire crew filming since 2003, with a
greenscreen studio and white infinity cove at Stevenage Arts & Leisure Centre
for anything that's easier indoors. Same team plans it, shoots it and edits it,
so nothing gets lost in the handover between three companies.

If you genuinely can't tell which page you want, ignore all of them and just
tell us what's happening. We've been doing this long enough to recognise a
case study wearing a promo's clothing.
