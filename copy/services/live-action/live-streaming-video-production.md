### 2 — Body

**H2: The clever bit is everything that doesn't go wrong**

Streaming looks simple right up to the moment the venue's guest wi-fi is asked
to carry 1080p to eight hundred people. Then it's a room full of faces watching
a spinning wheel, and nobody remembers the speaker afterwards.

So we plan for the failure first. Hard-wired connection where the venue has
one, a bonded mobile backup where it doesn't, and a check of the actual upload
speed on site rather than the number in the venue's brochure. Multiple cameras
run into a vision-mixing desk, so what goes out is cut live — wide for the
room, close for the speaker, and slides taken cleanly from the laptop rather
than filmed off a screen at an angle.

We handle the interactive parts too: moderated Q&A, polls and chat, plus a
producer whose only job is watching the outgoing stream and talking to the
gallery. Remote contributors get a rehearsal beforehand, which is where most
dial-in disasters are prevented.

Afterwards you keep the recording. The same footage cuts into a highlights
film, so the stream isn't the only thing you get for the money.
