### 2 — Body

**H2: Let somebody else say the good bit**

There's a claim on your site right now that you'd struggle to prove — faster,
easier, better value. Coming from you it's marketing. Coming from a customer
sat in their own workshop, in their own words, with the machine running behind
them, it's evidence.

We don't script case studies. We turn up with two cameras, a lapel mic and a
list of about eight questions nobody sees in advance, and we let the
conversation go where it goes. The usable line almost always arrives around
minute twenty, once they've forgotten the camera is there and started talking
to us like a person.

Then the story needs somewhere to happen. A QinetiQ fellowship is a room and a
whiteboard; an apprenticeship at LSBU is a workshop, a commute and a first
pay slip. We film the doing as well as the telling, because a talking head on
its own is a podcast with worse lighting.

One shoot day usually yields the three-minute film for the sales deck, a
ninety-second web cut and four vertical clips for LinkedIn.
