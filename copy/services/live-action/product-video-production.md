### 2 — Body

**H2: The bit online shopping can't do**

In a shop, the customer picks it up. They feel the weight, open the lid, check
whether the hinge feels cheap. Online they get six photographs and a bullet
list, and then they either guess or leave.

A product film does the picking-up for them, and the numbers back it: shoppers
who watch one are roughly three times more likely to buy and around 50% less
likely to send it back. Returns are where the margin quietly disappears, so
showing the true size, the actual finish and how the thing opens is worth as
much as the extra sales.

We shoot at our Stevenage studio, where there's a white infinity cove for clean
catalogue work plus coloured lighting and a range of background surfaces when
the product needs some drama. Turntables, macro passes for texture and detail,
and slow moves that let a surface catch the light properly. If the product is
bolted to a floor or the size of a shed, we come to you instead.

Add a voiceover, on-screen callouts or 3D from a STEP file where the interesting
part is inside the casing.
