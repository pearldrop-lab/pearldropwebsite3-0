### 2 — Body

**H2: The message everyone needs and nobody reads**

Restructure, new system, new safety rule, someone brilliant retiring after
thirty years. It goes out as a 700-word email with three bullet points and an
attachment, and by Thursday half the workforce has heard a worse version of it
in the car park.

Video fixes the version-control problem. Everyone gets the same ninety seconds,
from the same person, with the same tone — including the shift that starts at
six and never makes the town-hall meeting. We film around your operation rather
than stopping it: a chief executive can be done in forty minutes if we prep
properly, and a site tour can be shot between deliveries.

The trick is not making it look like a hostage video. We'll coach nervous
speakers, use an autocue only where it genuinely helps, and cut in what people
are actually doing on the floor. Where the subject is a process or a set of
numbers, 2D animation often carries it better than a face — we make that
in-house too.

Whatever it is, it won't be Death By PowerPoint. That remains the house rule.
