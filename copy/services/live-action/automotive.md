### 2 — Body

**H2: Cars don't photograph themselves**

Half five in the morning, an empty stretch of road, and a low sun doing the
thing it only does for about forty minutes. That's usually where the shot you
remember comes from — not from a car park at two in the afternoon with a white
van reflected in the door.

Vehicles are unforgiving to film. Bodywork mirrors everything, badges catch the
wrong highlight, and interiors are a nightmare of contrast between a bright
windscreen and a dark footwell. We plan around all of it: tracking shots from a
camera car, gimbal moves for the walkaround, polarisers to kill the sky in the
paintwork, and detail passes on the stitching, the switchgear and the tread.

It isn't only sports cars. We've filmed a camper conversion for Hertford
Campers and made a bus company genuinely funny for Grant Palmer, including
racing one of their buses across town against a man on foot. Fleets, LCVs,
plant and public transport all sell better in motion.

Drone work is available where the CAA permissions allow it — we hold Specific
Category UKPDRA-01 and have been flying commercially since 2016.
