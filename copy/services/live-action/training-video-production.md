### 2 — Body

**H2: Teach it once, properly**

Every organisation has one person who really knows how the thing works, and a
rota of new starters who each get a slightly different version of it depending
on how busy that person was that morning. Then they leave, and the knowledge
walks out with them.

Film it and the standard stops drifting. Everyone sees the same demonstration,
in the right order, with the awkward bit shown from the angle where it actually
makes sense — which is usually over the shoulder rather than head-on. People
retain far more from watching than from reading; Dale's Cone of Experience has
been making that point since the 1940s and nobody has convincingly argued with
it.

We handle whichever end you need. The creative approach, the script, the
storyboards, the shoot, professional actors or voiceover artists if your own
staff would rather not, and the graphics that label the parts nobody can name.
We'll also coach your subject-matter expert into a decent presenter — it takes
about an hour and saves the whole edit.

Film at your premises, on location, or at our Stevenage studio against the
greenscreen when the background is a distraction.
