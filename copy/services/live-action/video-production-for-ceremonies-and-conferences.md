### 2 — Body

**H2: One night, no second takes, 400 people watching**

An awards ceremony is a live broadcast that happens to serve dinner. The
winner's face lasts about two seconds, the acceptance speech overruns, and the
one camera you didn't budget for is the one that would have caught the table
going up. We cover the room properly: multiple broadcast-quality cameras,
radio mics on the podium, and a feed that plugs straight into the venue's
vision-mixing desk for the screens.

The bit most organisers underestimate is the content that plays before the
envelope. Our "Meet the Nominee" films are short documentary interviews shot in
each finalist's own workplace, so the room is already invested by the time the
category is read out. Finalists share them, sponsors get proper billing, and
nobody has to sit through a static list of names.

We've made ceremony and conference content for the Hertfordshire Chamber of
Commerce, biz4Biz and the Borough of Broxbourne, and our work has run on
screens at Mobile World Congress in Barcelona and at events across the US,
Australia, the Middle East and Europe.
