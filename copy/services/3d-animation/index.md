### 2 — Body

**H2: What do an unbuilt fire station and a transistor have in common?**

Neither can be filmed. One won't exist for two years; the other is a fraction of
a millimetre across and buried under a heat sink. A camera fails at both ends of
the scale, and no amount of clever lighting fixes that.

3D does. We do it two ways. Send us CAD and we'll render it — STEP files in,
photoreal product film out, with the geometry exactly as your engineers drew it.
Or we build the world from scratch: environments with weather, natural light and
physics that behave, from a cityscape down to the inside of a circuit board.

What you get that footage can't give you is access. Cutaways through a sealed
assembly. Exploded views that would mean destroying a unit. A camera move that
would otherwise need a crane, a road closure and a wall taken out. And because
it's all one build, the stills for the brochure come out of the same scene as
the film — one cost, two deliverables.

It suits manufacturers, engineering firms and public bodies planning something
big. Hertfordshire County Council used it to show what their future fire
stations and training centre would look like, years before anyone poured a
foundation.
