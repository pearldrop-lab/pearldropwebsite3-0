### 2 — Body

**H2: There is no location scout for the inside of a lung.**

We have driven a very long way for a good location. We have also stood in a car
park in February, being told by a lovely man in a hi-vis that the building we
came to film won't exist until 2027. Some places you simply cannot book.

So we build them. Whole environments live inside our rendering software:
cities, forests, rivers, coastlines, with weather, natural light that moves
through the day, and physics that behave the way an audience expects. Water
should slosh. Dust should hang. Get either wrong and everyone notices without
knowing why.

It works at the other end of the scale too. We go microscopic — through the
human body, or down onto the tiniest transistor on a circuit board — and hold a
continuous move from a skyline to a solder joint, which is not a shot you can
plan with a drone and a macro lens.

Practically, that means BIM files turned into walkthroughs, exploded diagrams
that come apart mid-sentence, and presentations that survive the graveyard slot
after lunch. Hertfordshire County Council used it to show their future fire
stations and training centre standing, finished and lit, long before the site
was cleared.
