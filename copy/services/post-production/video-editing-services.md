### 2 — Body

**H2: We'll admit it — this is the bit we argue about**

Not with clients. With each other, in the edit suite, over whether a shot earns
its four seconds. It's the same argument every time and it is the reason the
finished film moves. An edit is mostly deletion, and being ruthless on your
behalf is the actual service you're buying.

Here's what turns up on our drives: a conference filmed on two cameras, a
phone clip of the bit that mattered, an old brand film from 2019 nobody can
open any more, and a folder of stills. Our system will ingest almost anything
from almost any camera, so you don't need to know what a codec is to send it.

From there it's the unglamorous craft. Cleaning up the sound so the room noise
stops changing between takes. Grading so your MD's face is the same colour in
shot four as in shot forty. Adding music, titles and lower-thirds. Cutting
until nothing is left that you'd skip.

You can also just hand us something you already own. Retooling old footage into
a fresh cut is usually the cheapest video you'll make all year.
