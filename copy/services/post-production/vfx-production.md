### 2 — Body

**H2: What if the shot you need doesn't exist?**

Sometimes the location is on another continent, the factory floor has a
competitor's logo on the wall, or the brief cheerfully includes a 50ft battle
robot. That's when the job stops being a shoot and starts being a VFX job.

We work in Adobe After Effects with a shelf of specialist plugins, and we have
a greenscreen in our own Hertfordshire studio — which means we can put a
presenter in a data centre, a warehouse or a snowstorm without anybody leaving
Stevenage or filling in a risk assessment for the snowstorm.

Plenty of the work is quieter than that, and more useful. Removing a sign that
shouldn't be in frame. Blurring faces, badges and number plates so a film clears
legal. Building the graphics, charts and animated titles that carry the numbers
your voiceover is trying to explain. Cleaning up a shot that was almost right.

Send us raw footage and we'll tell you honestly what can be fixed in post and
what would be faster to reshoot. Both answers save money; only one of them is
the one you were hoping for.
