Why go up

Some things simply cannot be photographed from the ground. The whole site in one frame. A building in its landscape. A showground at the moment it fills up. That is what the camera goes up for.

One flight. Every angle.

We don't go up for a single shot. One visit gives you the opening reveal, the stills, the context and the detail — cut, graded and delivered with the rest of the job.
