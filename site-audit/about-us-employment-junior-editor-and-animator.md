# Junior Editor and Animator

## JOB POSTING

## Date of posting: 16/10/23

## Role: Junior Animator and Editor - office-based

This is an amazing opportunity for somebody with a passion for animation and editing, a creative eye and who wants to be part of a growing production company with big ambitions. You’ll be working with a dynamic, busy, fun and professional team – currently 5 people. You’ll be creating eye-catching 2D explainer and character animations. You’ll also be making animated intro/outro boards, lower thirds and animated logos for every project we work on, as well as editing some of the live-action footage shot by our team.

The clients you’ll be working with cover a diverse range of industries and come from across the UK and beyond. We create high-quality and engaging content that clients are proud to share and that brings them proven results. You could find yourself working on a variety of projects for a wide range of clients –from drink brands to automotive companies.

## About Pearldrop

Established in 2003, Pearldrop is a full-service creative film agency based in Stevenage, covering the whole of the UK and beyond. Our services include production, editing, animation, aerial photography and videography and stills.

[Find out more about us here.](https://pearldrop.com/about-us/)

## The Opportunity

Reports to: Denise Austin, Director

Location: Stevenage Arts & Leisure Centre, Lytton Way, Stevenage, Hertfordshire, SG1 1LZ

Role includes:

After Effects production of explainer and character animations, intros, outros and lower thirds

Editing using Adobe Premiere

Understanding clients’ requirement from discussions and briefs and generating animation ideas

Developing storyboards where necessary

Creative input into projects

We anticipate approximately 75% of your time will be spent on animation and After Effects and 25% editing.
