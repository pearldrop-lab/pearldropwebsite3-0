## AI Gallery

Here’s a collection of the AI images we’ve created with the amazing Midjourney. Along with each gallery, you’ll find the prompt that we used to create the image.

## As detailed as your imagination

We’ve started to use AI in many areas of our business. One of its most exciting uses for a creative video production agency like ours is image generation, We’ve been using [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com) to create some truly astonishing imagery, both in photo-real and more expressionist artistic styles. The results are already appearing in our proposal documents and, increasingly, in work that we deliver to clients. Below are some of our recent generations.
