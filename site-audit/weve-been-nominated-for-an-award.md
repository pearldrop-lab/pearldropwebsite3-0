# We’ve been nominated for an award!

![Herts Young Homeless: Beth's Story](https://pearldrop.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Beths-story-1.jpg)

- December 20, 2023
- Simon

We’ve got some fantastic news to share with you!

We’re delighted to announce that an animation we created earlier this year for [Herts Young Homeless](https://www.hyh.org.uk/) is up for a Smiley Charity Film Award! It’s a prestigious national recognition programme for charities, with the ceremony taking place on March 20th 2024 at the Leicester Square Odeon Luxe cinema. Please take a few minutes to watch the animation, and, if you can, vote for us using this link:

[https://smileycharityfilmawards.com/films/beths-st](https://smileycharityfilmawards.com/films/beths-story) [ory](https://smileycharityfilmawards.com/films/beths-story)

We’re really proud of the partnership we’ve developed with Herts Young Homeless, and are so happy to see our work together receiving recognition at national level.

Fingers crossed!
