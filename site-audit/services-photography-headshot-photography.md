# Headshot photography

#### Be your best self

A great headshot is more important today than ever before. You need to look professional and approachable on LinkedIn and all of your other business social media accounts.

It’s also important for your customers to see your team on your website. It establishes trust and credibility, and really helps your customers to know they’re dealing with real people.

You can choose to carry out your shoot at our studio in Stevenage. Or we can come to your office. It’s totally up to you. Either way, we can offey you a choice of backgrounds, including white, black and grey, as well as all kinds of textures.

We know how hard it can be to have your photograph taken professionally. This is why we make sure your time with us during the shoot is as relaxing as possible. We’ll spend the first few minutes speaking with you, ans not photographing at all. This is so we can get to know each other and you can relax into the process.

Once we start photographing, you don’t need to worry about how to stand or sit. We’ll tell you exactly how to pose. We’ll keep the atmosphere light and make sure you’re relaxed and happy at all times.

After the shoot, we’ll send you a polished set of final images. And don’t worry: we always give your photographs a final polish, so you’ll look your best in every one.
