# Lifestyle photography

#### Enhance your reality

Lifestyle photography is an excellent way to give your customers a feel for your company, your products and your services.

You can ask us to supply some models to be in your photoshoot. Or, if you prefer, you and your team can take part. Either way, we’ll carefully stage your location so that it looks its best, and we’ll keep a close eye on clothing and makeup to make sure everything’s looking tip-top.

Lifestyle photography is perfect for brochures and magazine articles where you want to show off your products and services in a relaxed, realistic way. You’ll give your customers an excellent insight into your products and services, but without being too overtly salesy about it.

If you’d like to take part in your own shoot, don’t worry about posing or knowing where to stand. We’ll give you simple directions to follow, so all you you have to do is enjoy yourself.

Lifestyle photography shoots tend to come in two forms. They involve: a) staying in one location and carefully lighting and arranging everything, or b) going out and about with you and capturing enhanced versions of reality as events unfold.

You’ll end up with a set of high-quality images, fully retouched and full of sparkle. We always deliver our lifestyle photographs in two formats: 1) low-res (under 2mb each) for social media and web use, and 2) high-res originals for perfect physical printing.
