# What makes 2D animation a great choice for your business?

![2D animation example](https://pearldrop.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/2D-animation-example-1.jpg)

- April 17, 2023
- Simon

Here at Pearldrop we love 2D animation – it’s always fun and we get to be super creative.

We all watched cartoons when we were kids and they’re a familiar format.

But what makes 2D animation so beneficial for businesses?

1) DIGESTABLE

You can include a large amount of information in a short animation, and people find it easy to take in and retain that information.

2) TAILORABLE

If you can think it, we can create it! You want a scene with a red house, blue trees in the garden, a pink moon and a yellow car outside? No problem!

3) TIME-EFFICIENT FOR YOU

We can create the animation from our studio, which means we don’t have to spend time finding a suitable date, time and location for filming. And you don’t have to spend time getting your space ready for us to film in.

4) NO NEED TO BE CAMERA-SHY

If your team members are reluctant to be on camera, then creating an animation means they don’t have to be. We can still turn them into animated characters, though.

5) EASY TO UPDATE

If a statistic, date, or fact changes in six months’ time, it’s easy to replace and doesn’t mean refilming. This means you can refresh and update regularly.

6) RECOGNISABLE

We can use your branding and colours throughout, which makes it easily recognisable as material produced by your organisation. Great for increasing brand awareness.

7) A USEFUL SEO TOOL

People are likely to watch a short animation packed full of information and attractive imagery right through to the end. The more people who watch your animation through to the end, the better its ranking will be.

If you’re interested in how 2D animation can give your organisation a boost, then do get in contact. You can be sure of an animated response from us!
