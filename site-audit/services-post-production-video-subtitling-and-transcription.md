![Subtitling](https://pearldrop.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Subtitling.-t-1024x578.jpg)

#### Make your message clear

We strongly recommend you make subtitles available on every video you publish.

YouTube allows the viewer to choose whether or not to display subtitles. And with the right settings, you can even offer subtitles in multiple languages.

Subtitles are also crucial for social media. Most people watch videos on social media with the sound turned off, so if there’s any speaking at all, they just won’t hear it. So subtitles mean you’re making sure your messages reach the most people possible.

If you’d like to display subtitles on your videos in languages other than English, we work with several transcription partners and can supply a quote on request.
