# Sustainability

Our journey to Net Zero

## Here's how we're working to be a more sustainable company

Protecting the environment and the future of our planet is important to Pearldrop. With this in mind, we’re pleased to say that we’ve completed the Silver module of [Wenta’s](https://wenta.co.uk/actionzero) Action Zero programme, and are on our way to Gold.

![Net Zero silver award](https://pearldrop.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Wenta-Silver-Badge-1024x380.jpg)

## The steps we're taking

Alongside participating in the Action Zero programme here are some of the things we’ve done on the journey so far.

The Pearldrop team measured their carbon footprint using the WWF carbon footprint calculator and all received tips on how we can reduce our carbon footprint. We can then regularly calculate our score and see if changes we’re making are having a positive impact on our carbon footprint. You’ll find the calculator here if you’d like to have a go: [https://footprint.wwf.org.uk/](https://footprint.wwf.org.uk/)

We’re planting a tree in the Forest of Marston Vale for every member of the Pearldrop team and will do every quarter. Find out more here: [https://www.marstonvale.org/](https://www.marstonvale.org/)
