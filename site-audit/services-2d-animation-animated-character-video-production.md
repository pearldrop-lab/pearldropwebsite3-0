# Animated character videos

#### Add some personality

Using a 2D animated character as a presenter in your video is an awesome way to make your message more relatable.

2D animated characters can be fun or serious. They can be humanoid, animal or any kind of object. They can talk directly to your audience, or act as protagonists in scenes that are accompanied by a voiceover.

Pearldrop uses amazing performance-capturing technology to give realistic motion to our animated characters. So if you’re ever in our studio and you see an editor waving his or her hands around and grinning at the screen, there’s a good chance he or she is animating a character.

Click [here](https://pearldrop.com/what-makes-2d-animation-a-great-choice-for-your-business/) to check out our blog post about how animation can help your business.
