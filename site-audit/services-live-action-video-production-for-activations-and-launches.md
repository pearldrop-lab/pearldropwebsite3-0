# Video production for activations and launches

#### Keep the magic alive

Maximise customer engagement with your brand after your activation or roadshow by sending them the film of the event.

We’ll capture all of the colourful, exciting and entertaining moments that make your activation uniquely yours. With our steadicam and timelapse cameras, we’ll make a film that’s as individual as your brand.

We love to capture the sound of activations as well as the pictures. Got live actors interacting with the public? We’ll work with them to give us a special performance for the film.

If you’re looking for a photographer for your event, we offer a convenient package that provides both video and stills. Why book two companies when you can just book us?
