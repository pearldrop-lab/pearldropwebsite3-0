## JOSCAR Accreditation

JOSCAR (the Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register) is a collaborative tool used by a growing number of prime contractors in the aerospace,

defence and security industry to act as a single repository for pre-qualification and compliance information.

Pearldrop is extremely pleased to say that we are fully JOSCAR-accredited.

![Hertfordshire Chamber of Commerce patrons](https://pearldrop.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/patron-logo.jpg)

## Patrons of the Hertfordshire Chamber of Commerce

As long-standing members of the Hertfordshire business community, Pearldrop have taken an active part in the Hertfordshire Chamber of Commerce and Industry since 2010.

In 2012, we became Patrons of the Hertfordshire Chamber, and have been honoured to be able to supply all of their video and photographic content ever since.
